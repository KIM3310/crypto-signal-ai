# Monetization Playbook - crypto-signal-ai

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2C/B2B research automation
- **Primary buyer:** Quant learners, research communities, analytics teams, and automation builders.
- **Revenue wedge:** Auditable signal research with indicators, sentiment hooks, and backtesting, without return promises.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Paid research dashboard** - pricing hypothesis: $9-$29/month research access
2. **Private strategy-lab template** - pricing hypothesis: $1k-$5k setup
3. **Automation/report workflow setup** - pricing hypothesis: $500-$3k/month private reporting

## Acquisition Channels

- Research newsletter
- Backtest transparency posts
- n8n/Slack automation templates

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Tests pass
- Backtest assumptions visible
- Financial-advice boundary is explicit

## Risk Boundaries

- No investment advice
- No guaranteed returns
- Live trading requires risk controls

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish transparent sample report
- **60 days:** Add disclaimer-first landing copy
- **90 days:** Offer automation setup

## Metrics That Matter

- Report retention
- Backtest reproducibility
- Automation adoption

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
